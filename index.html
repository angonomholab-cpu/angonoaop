Priority 5: Disease Control Targets

Proactive deployment of diagnostic kits and surveillance commodities for communicable diseases.

Targeting 500 presumptive patients via active case finding and deploying 1,000 TB Management Kits.

Maintaining Dengue awareness in schools and continuing the Zero-Malaria sustainability program across barangays.

HIV & STI Testing

Priority 6: Frontliners & Infrastructure

Investing in human capital and facility upgrades to strengthen local health systems.

Empowering Workforce

  • Targeting 100% of BHWs, BNS, and Job Orders to receive vital SSS and PhilHealth benefits.
  • Hiring allocation for 1 Dentist IV, 1 Dental Admin, and 10 new HRH personnel.

Modernizing Facilities

  • Proposed comprehensive renovation of the Angono Logistics & Supply Management Unit.
  • Acquisition of 5 new ICT Equipment units to support electronic medical records capability.
