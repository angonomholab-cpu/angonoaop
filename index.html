Priority 5: Disease Control Targets

Executing aggressive active case finding protocols and localized diagnostic capability enhancement for communicable diseases.

Tuberculosis

Targeting 500 presumptive patients via mobile x-rays and proposing 1,000 TB Management Kits.

Dengue & Malaria

Sustaining Dengue awareness in schools and maintaining Zero-Malaria sustainability initiatives.

HIV & STI

Procuring 600 Syphilis & HIV Test Kits and establishing local HIV Viral Load testing.

Priority 6: Frontliners & Infra

Optimizing service delivery by expanding human resource capacities, ensuring worker welfare, and modernizing health infrastructure.

Workforce Expansion

Proposing the hiring of 1 Dentist IV, 1 Dental Admin, and 10 new HRH personnel to augment primary care networks.

Welfare & Infrastructure

Allocating vital SSS & PhilHealth benefits for 100% of BHWs, BNS, and Job Orders. Upgrading ICT equipment for digital medical records.
