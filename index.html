Tuberculosis

Targeting 500 presumptive patients via active case finding and proposing 1,000 TB Management Kits.

Sustaining Dengue awareness campaigns in schools and maintaining the Zero-Malaria sustainability initiatives.

HIV & STI

Proposing 600 Syphilis Test Kits, 600 HIV Test Kits, and procuring local HIV Viral Load testing.

Empowering our Workforce

Modernizing Facilities

  • Renovation of the Angono Logistics & Supply Management Unit.
  • Acquisition of 5 new ICT Equipment units for electronic medical records.
