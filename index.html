Priority 4: Frontliners & Infrastructure

Empowering our Workforce

  • 100% Worker Benefits: Full provision of SSS and PhilHealth benefits for 134 personnel (BHWs, BNS, and Job Orders).
  • Capacity Expansion: New hiring allocation for 3 Medical Officers, 3 Nurses, 2 Midwives, 1 MedTech, 1 Dentist IV, and support staff.
  • Technical Training: Focused seminars on Disease Surveillance and Emerging Infectious Diseases.

Modernizing Facilities

  • Logistics Center: Planned renovation of the Angono Logistics & Supply Management Unit to secure medical stockpiles.
  • Digital Integration: Acquisition of 5 new ICT Desktop/Laptop units to support interoperable electronic medical records across barangays.
  • Disaster Resilience: Equipping the Evacuation Center with essential machinery (Generators, AC units, Carts).
