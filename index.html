Priority 5: Disease Control

Surveillance and Active Case Finding (CY 2027)

Tuberculosis

Aggressive targeting of 500 presumptive patients via active case finding (mobile X-rays) and procurement of 1,000 TB Kits (₱8.5M).

Dengue & Malaria

Procuring 100 rolls of treated screens for dengue prevention and maintaining Zero-Malaria sustainability protocols.

HIV & STI

Deployment of 600 Syphilis and 600 HIV rapid test kits, paired with local HIV Viral Load Point of Care testing capabilities.
