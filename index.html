Priority 5: Disease Control Targets

Strengthening our surveillance and rapid response capabilities against infectious diseases, focusing on Tuberculosis, Dengue, and HIV/STI.

Tuberculosis Control

  • Active case finding via mobile x-rays for 500 presumptive patients.
  • Procurement of 1,000 TB Management Kits to ensure uninterrupted medication.

Dengue & Malaria

  • Sustaining Dengue awareness and prevention campaigns across all schools.
  • Maintaining commodities for the Zero-Malaria sustainability initiative.

HIV & STI Surveillance

Proposing the procurement of 600 Syphilis Test Kits, 600 HIV Test Kits, 3,000 lubricants, and local HIV Viral Load Point of Care testing.
