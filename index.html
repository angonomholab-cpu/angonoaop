Priority 1: Expanded Immunization (EPI)

Protecting Angono's infants and children against vaccine-preventable diseases is our top priority. The 2027 AOP focuses on securing vital vaccines to ensure zero outbreaks and robust herd immunity across all barangays.

Key Impact:

  • Reduces under-5 child mortality rates.
  • Prevents local community outbreaks of Polio and Measles.

Priority 3: Oral Health Programs

To promote early intervention and preventative dental care, the AOP outlines the widespread distribution of complete dental kits to children and adults in the community.

We are also proposing the upgrade of clinical equipment to ensure our dentists can provide safe, sterile, and effective ambulatory care.
