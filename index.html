Priority 3: Oral Health Programs

Primary Dental Care Access

Upgrading clinical equipment and sustaining preventative dental outreach for children and adults.

  • Dental Kits: Procurement of 1,500 Kid's Dental Kits and 1,500 Adult Kits for community missions.
  • Clinical Upgrades: Purchasing new Autoclave Sterilizers, Ultrasonic Scalers, and Surgical Forceps.
  • Preventative: Deployment of Pits & Fissure sealants across target barangays.
  • Recognition: Province-wide awarding program for 300 Orally Fit Children.

Priority 5: Disease Control

Aggressive Surveillance & Testing

Targeted funding for active case finding, rapid testing kits, and community-level infectious disease containment.

Tuberculosis Control

Targeting 500 presumptive patients via active case finding (mobile X-rays) and procuring 1,000 TB Management Kits (₱8.5M).

STI & Vector Surveillance

Procurement of 600 Syphilis & HIV rapid test kits, local HIV Viral Load testing, and 100 rolls of treated screens for Dengue prevention.

Priority 6: Frontliners & Infra

Empowering the Workforce

Investing in the welfare of our human resources for health and modernizing logistical infrastructure.

  • Worker Benefits: Guaranteeing SSS and PhilHealth benefits for 100% of 134 BHWs, BNS, and Job Orders (₱2.41M).
  • HR Expansion: Hiring budget for 3 MOs, 3 Nurses, 2 Midwives, 1 MedTech, 1 Dentist IV, and 1 Dental Admin.
  • Infrastructure: Renovation of the Logistics Unit and acquisition of 5 new ICT equipment sets for records.
